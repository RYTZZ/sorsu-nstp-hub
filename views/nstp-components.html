What is NSTP?

The National Service Training Program (NSTP) is a civic education and defense preparedness program implemented in higher education institutions in the Philippines. It is designed to enhance civic consciousness and defense preparedness in the youth through training in military science, literacy, civic welfare, and other programs.

Three Components of NSTP

🎖️ Reserve Officers' Training Corps (ROTC)

▼

Purpose

ROTC is designed to provide military training to tertiary-level students to motivate, train, organize, and mobilize them for national defense preparedness.

Key Activities

  • Basic Military Training
  • Physical Fitness and Drills
  • Leadership Development
  • National Security Awareness
  • Disaster Preparedness Training

Duration

Two (2) semesters of training

🤝 Civic Welfare Training Service (CWTS)

▼

Purpose

CWTS is designed to contribute to the general welfare and betterment of life for the members of the community, especially those who are underprivileged, homeless, or indigenous.

Key Activities

  • Community Immersion Programs
  • Health and Sanitation Projects
  • Environmental Conservation
  • Livelihood Programs
  • Educational Assistance
  • Values Formation

Duration

Two (2) semesters of training

📖 Literacy Training Service (LTS)

▼

Purpose

LTS is designed to train students to teach literacy and numeracy skills to school children, out-of-school youth, and other segments of society in need of their service.

Key Activities

  • Teaching Basic Literacy
  • Numeracy Skills Training
  • Alternative Learning System (ALS)
  • Tutorial Programs
  • Reading Comprehension Enhancement
  • Life Skills Education

Duration

Two (2) semesters of training

🎯 NSTP Thrusts

1. Civic Consciousness

Developing awareness of social issues and civic responsibility

2. Defense Preparedness

Training for national security and emergency response

3. Community Service

Active participation in community development programs

4. Values Formation

Fostering patriotism, discipline, and ethical values

📋 NSTP Requirements at SorSU

  • Enrollment in any NSTP component for two consecutive semesters
  • Regular attendance (minimum of 80% per semester)
  • Active participation in all activities and projects
  • Completion of required community service hours
  • Submission of all required reports and documentation
  • Passing grade in both NSTP 1 and NSTP 2
